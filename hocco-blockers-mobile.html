Blockers

◔

Disputed

A blocker is a claim until the named party acknowledges it. Unresolved disputes escalate to both HODs after 2–3 days — both, never one chain, because one chain implies fault.

Open & acknowledged

Acknowledged means the delay is agreed, not that anyone is at fault.

Days accumulate against a step, never against a person or a team. No blocker on this screen rolls up into a score.
